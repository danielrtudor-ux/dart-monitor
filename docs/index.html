METHODOLOGY

How to read the screen

TTM uses the latest full year plus the current cumulative interim period, less the comparable prior-year interim period. Annual fallback is labelled when a complete bridge is unavailable.

Blank values are deliberate. The screen withholds a ratio when debt, cash restrictions, share-class claims, currency conversion or the underlying profit measure is unsuitable.

Quality reflects extraction and methodology checks. “Clean” means no current audit flag; it is not an investment recommendation or a statement that earnings are normalized.

EV includes reviewed debt and a book-value proxy for noncontrolling interests. Financial companies use P/E, P/B and ROE; industrial EV and DCF are withheld.
